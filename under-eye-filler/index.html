GOOD TO KNOW

Under-Eye Filler questions, answered.

What can under-eye filler help with?
It may help selected cases of under-eye hollowing or support-related shadowing. It does not directly treat every cause of dark circles or puffiness.
Who may not be a good candidate?
People with prominent under-eye bags, certain skin or medical conditions, or concerns unlikely to improve with filler may need a different approach. Only an in-person assessment can determine suitability.
Will it remove dark circles?
Dark circles can have several causes, including pigmentation, visible vessels, shadowing, or skin quality. Filler may only help when hollowing or structure contributes to the appearance.
What are the risks?
Swelling, bruising, lumps, asymmetry, or prolonged puffiness can occur. Fillers also have rare but serious blood-vessel and vision-related risks. Your provider will review these carefully.
