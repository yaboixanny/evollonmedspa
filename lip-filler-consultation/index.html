GOOD TO KNOW

Lip Filler questions, answered.

What can lip filler change?
Lip filler may add shape, border definition, balance, or fullness. Your provider can explain what may be realistic for your anatomy and goals.
Can lip filler look natural?
A subtle result may be possible depending on your starting features, the treatment plan, and how your body responds. Explain the look you prefer during consultation.
What should I expect afterward?
Tenderness, swelling, firmness, and bruising are common after treatment and usually improve with time. Your provider will explain aftercare and when to seek urgent help.
How long does lip filler last?
Duration varies by person, product type, amount, and metabolism. Your provider can explain expected timing without guaranteeing a specific duration.
