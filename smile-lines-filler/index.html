GOOD TO KNOW

Smile Lines Filler questions, answered.

What are smile lines?
Smile lines are the folds that run from the sides of the nose toward the corners of the mouth. They are a normal facial feature and may become more noticeable over time.
Can filler soften smile lines?
Filler may soften selected folds by adding support or volume, but results depend on anatomy and the cause of the crease. Your provider will assess the surrounding face before recommending treatment.
Will my smile still look natural?
The goal and amount of change are part of your consultation. Your provider can discuss a conservative plan designed around your facial movement and preferences.
What are the risks?
Possible effects include tenderness, swelling, bruising, lumps, or asymmetry. Rare but serious blood-vessel complications can occur. Your provider will explain warning signs and aftercare.
